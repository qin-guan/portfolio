## Why Astro for a blog?

VitePress is excellent for docs, but a dated post stream with tags, drafts, and RSS is more natural as an Astro **content collection**.

Frontmatter on this post:

- `title` — Hello from Astro
- `description` — short summary for listings and SEO
- `date` — 2026-10-04
